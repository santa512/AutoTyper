# Auto Typer 0.3.3

- Live captions are much more accurate. The new **Accurate** mode, now the default, uses Whisper `small.en` and adds punctuation and capitals. It handles accents, fast speech, background music and names far better.
- Captions are split at natural pauses. While someone is talking you see a dim preview ending in "…", which is replaced by the final phrase about 1–3 seconds after they pause.
- **Fast** mode keeps the previous engine for word-by-word captions with a smaller download. Switch between them in the caption toolbar; the choice is remembered.
- Everything still runs on your PC. No audio is uploaded.

The first time Accurate captions start, Auto Typer downloads the speech model (about 190 MB) from the official whisper.cpp repository and verifies its SHA-256. Later starts work offline. On older or low-power PCs, captions may arrive a little later; switch to Fast if they fall behind.
