# Auto Typer 0.3

The recommended x64 app gains fully local microphone-to-headphones voice preview.

## Audio panel

Select a microphone and headphones in the left **Audio** panel, then click **Start preview**. Adjust **Depth**, **Body** and **Preview volume**, or switch **Compare** to hear the original voice. Stop preview or collapse the panel to release the audio devices. Preview is always off when the app starts.

Depth is restricted to a subtle 0–3 semitone range and uses formant compensation to retain a natural vocal tone. Local warmth EQ, gentle dynamics and output limiting add body. Settings start at 1 semitone, 45% body and 35% preview volume; final results depend on your voice and microphone.

Processing uses bundled MIT-licensed Signalsmith Stretch/Linear and NAudio WASAPI. No cloud service, API key, GPU, model download or virtual audio driver is needed. Audio is not recorded or uploaded. This version is a headphone preview and does not change the microphone used by other apps. Estimated stream delay and input/output meters appear in the panel.
