## What's new

- **Clipboard paste in both editions:** load text without starting a typing run.
- **QR scanning:** drag a region and load its decoded text into the input.
- **English text extraction:** local PaddleOCR PP-OCRv5 recognition, with English models included. No Python, GPU or model download is required.
- **Screenshots:** select a region and Save As PNG, or copy the image when the shared clipboard toggle is on. Windows notifications can include a thumbnail when supported.
- **Instant Share:** connect your own Cloudflare R2 bucket, capture a region, and place its public URL in the input and clipboard. Sharing stays disabled until configured. Credentials are encrypted for your Windows user, rather than bundled in the EXE.
- **Share history:** a scrollable panel with upload dates, estimated 14-day expiry, and Copy/Open icons. The panel leaves the main toolbar accessible. History is encrypted locally and retains the 200 most recent links.
- **Settings:** vertical navigation for Auto Typer, Cloudflare and Hotkeys on one scrollable page. Preferences persist between launches. Back and Save sit together; an inline Yes/No prompt handles unsaved edits.
- **Five global shortcuts:** separate bindings for typing start/pause, Screenshot, Instant Share, QR and Text Extract. Tooltips and the tray menu show available shortcuts. Conflicts are reported and duplicate bindings are rejected.
- **Typing display:** a thin full-width progress line and current-character highlighting. The main app's default ready delay is now 3 seconds; compatible saved preferences are preserved.
- **Single instance:** reopening the main app restores the existing window, including copies launched from another folder, and avoids duplicate hotkey registrations.
- **Optional tray mode:** Minimize and Close keep the app running. The themed, topmost menu includes Main, QR, OCR, Screenshot, Instant Share, Settings and Exit, with icons and available shortcuts. Its position is constrained above the taskbar, without a white frame or visible scrollbar.

## Before upgrading

Choose **Exit** from the old app's tray menu before opening v0.2. Closing a tray-enabled window only hides it. Existing compatible settings and local share history are retained. Run one edition at a time; Compact is a separate application.

Only Instant Share uploads a screenshot. Other screen tools process the selected region locally. Configure your R2 bucket with a **14-day expiration rule for `share/`**. Deletion is handled by R2 and can be asynchronous; history expiry dates are estimates. The app does not overwrite bucket lifecycle rules or include a shared storage account's credentials.

Compact remains focused on typing, with its existing 2.5-second delay default and Ctrl/Alt/Shift hotkeys. The new screen tools, settings page, single-instance guard and tray menu belong to the recommended app.

The executables are unsigned. Windows may show an unknown-publisher or reputation prompt. Verify downloads with their supplied checksums.
