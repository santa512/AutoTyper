Windows 10 (1809+) or Windows 11 · x64 / ARM64 · Portable or MSI setup. The main app reopens its existing window. Run one edition at a time to avoid shortcut conflicts.

01

A pace that fits

Adjust speed and rhythm, with pauses around punctuation and a default 1% mistake chance. The current character and a thin progress line show where you are.

02

Capture and scan

Drag to select a screen region. Save a screenshot as PNG, copy the image, or scan a QR code straight into your text input.

03

Extract English text

Turn a selected screen region into editable text. English recognition runs locally, with its models included in the download.

04

Share a screenshot

Connect your own Cloudflare R2 bucket once. Instant Share puts the image URL into your input and clipboard, with a history of links. Shared screenshots are saved for 14 days using your bucket’s lifecycle rule.

05

Keep tools in the tray

Minimize or close to the tray and keep your shortcuts active. Enable Run on startup to launch at Windows sign-in. The themed tray menu brings capture, QR, text extraction and sharing within reach.

06

Translate and back up

Connect Microsoft Translator, choose a language and translate clipboard text with Ctrl+Shift+T. Keep the original and translation together. Export settings and API keys in a password-encrypted backup.

FROM DOWNLOAD TO FIRST KEYSTROKE

Ready in a few steps.

Open a portable EXE, or choose MSI setup to install.

  1. 1

    Download and open

    Download AutoTyper.exe and double-click it. The first launch may take a moment.

  2. 2

    Add your text

    Paste into the input box. Open Settings to choose your pace, rhythm and ready delay, which defaults to 3 seconds.

  3. 3

    Focus the destination

    Press Start, then click your destination text field during the countdown. Use Ctrl+Shift+Space to pause or resume.

A few things to know.

Use a plain text field for predictable corrections, and keep the destination caret in place. Automatic formatting and indentation can affect output.

Switching foreground windows stops the run. Enter and Tab keep the destination app’s normal behavior. Apps running with higher privileges may reject simulated input.

The app does not read destination text or record your physical typing. The main app saves settings between launches. Only Instant Share uploads a screenshot, to the bucket you configure. Read the full guide ↗
